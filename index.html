How to earn 5 stars (without ever asking for them)
  • Own it. "I will take care of this" beats "that is not my department" every time.
  • Sound human. Smile before you speak. Slightly slower and calmer than normal.
  • Use their name two or three times, naturally.
  • Solve it on this call if at all possible. If not, give a clear next step and a time.
  • Recap at the end. Customers who know exactly what happens next rate you higher.
  • End on something positive: "Have a wonderful holiday!" Warm, specific, unhurried.
  • Never ask for, hint at or coach a score. The experience does the work.


Your 5 golden rules
  • Never guess. "Let me double-check that for you" is always a good answer.
  • Never promise what you cannot guarantee (compensation, room requests, refunds, timings).
  • Always say the full cost before you change anything.
  • Verify first, every single time, even if the notes say it was already done.
  • Safety, visas, passports: point to GOV.UK foreign travel advice. Never give your own view.

OBJECTIONS
An objection is the customer telling you what they need before they say yes.
Memorize the method: ACAA
  • Acknowledge: "I understand price matters." Never argue first.

  • Avoid the word "but". Use "and" or "so".

ANGRY CUSTOMERS
Stay calm, stay professional, stay in control. Lower your voice and slow down.
Memorize the method: H.E.A.R.D.
  • Hear them out: let them vent, no interrupting, take brief notes.
  • Empathise: name the feeling. "I can hear how frustrating this has been."

  • Resolve: clear options with timelines. "Here is what I can do..."
  • Deliver and follow up: do what you promised, note it, say what happens next.

SAYING NO WELL
A kind, clear no with a real alternative can still earn a great survey. Customers remember how it felt.
Memorize the 5 steps
  • 1 Acknowledge. Let them finish. "I can hear how frustrating this is, and I am sorry."
  • 2 Say the no early and kindly. Do not bury it. "Unfortunately I am not able to do that."
  • 3 Give the reason in their terms. "This keeps your details safe." "The airline sets this charge." Never "that is our policy".
  • 4 Lead with what you CAN do. One or two real options: "What I can do is..."
  • 5 Agree the next step. "Would that work for you?" Then say what happens next and by when, and note it.

CANCEL AND REBOOK, REINSTATEMENT
Two different processes. Reinstatement restores the same booking. Cancel and rebook creates a new one.
Every amendment or cancellation: ask these 3 questions, in this order
  • 1 How many days to departure? It sets the fees (Calc button, section F).
  • 2 Is there a Direct Debit? The icon next to the cost. An amendment can break it; a cancellation must leave it inactive (section P).
  • 3 Has the customer paid enough? If payments do not cover the cancellation fee, do not continue: ask for a payment, or leave the booking live and write memos only.
  • Cancelled under 24 hours ago? Reinstatement. Otherwise cancel and rebook.
ReinstatementCancel and rebook
What it isThe cancelled booking is brought back to life.Cancel the old booking and create a new booking with a new reference.
WhenOnly within 24 hours of the cancellation (no exceptions) and only if finances allow.When accommodation cannot be amended, or a Holiday Change booking is stamped with free cancellation.
Fee50 per person, mandatory. Tell the customer before you start.Amendment fees on the new booking (AFEE). Old amendment fees stay on the old booking.
IncentiveNone.Holiday Change only: Rebooking Incentive (RBI) up to 10% if the new holiday costs more.
MemosCRBK memos linking the bookings.CRBK / NREF on the OLD booking (new reference only). CRBK / OREF on the NEW booking (old reference only).

API"Your passport details we need so the airline can check you in."
SSR"A special request on the flight, such as assistance or a meal."
Errata"Any updates to the information in the brochure or on the website."
Manifest"The list of passengers on the flight."
Incident"A change to the holiday that is still being looked at."  (Do not mention it to the customer until Holiday Change has left notes.)
