TUI PRE TRAVEL
GUIDELINES

The lines you need to know by heart to handle a call with confidence. Learn it one colour at a time.

1OPENWarm greeting, your name, ask who you are speaking to.
2VERIFYSecurity check first. No booking details before it.
3DISCOVERAsk open questions, listen, repeat back what you heard.
4RESOLVEClear options, best first. Be honest about what you can and cannot do.
5ADD VALUEOne relevant extra, linked to what they told you.
6CONFIRMRecap actions, costs, timings, anything you promised.
7CLOSEAnything else? Thank them. Notes straight away.

How to earn 5 stars (without ever asking for them)

  • Own it. "I will take care of this" beats "that is not my department" every time.
  • Sound human. Smile before you speak. Slightly slower and calmer than normal.
  • Use their name two or three times, naturally.
  • Solve it on this call if at all possible. If not, give a clear next step and a time.
  • Recap at the end. Customers who know exactly what happens next rate you higher.
  • End on something positive: "Have a wonderful holiday!" Warm, specific, unhurried.
  • Never ask for, hint at or coach a score. The experience does the work.

Your 5 golden rules

  • Never guess. "Let me double-check that for you" is always a good answer.
  • Never promise what you cannot guarantee (compensation, room requests, refunds, timings).
  • Always say the full cost before you change anything.
  • Verify first, every single time, even if the notes say it was already done.
  • Safety, visas, passports: point to GOV.UK foreign travel advice. Never give your own view.

Memorize this one first

  • "Good morning/afternoon, thank you for calling TUI. You are through to [your name]. Who am I speaking to today?"
  • Use it on every call. Smile when you say it. Let them finish before you speak again.

VERIFY

Security check first. Always. Even if the notes say GDPR was already passed.

Memorize the three scenarios

  • GREEN (standard): "Can you confirm your full name please?" then "Thank you [name]. How can I help you today?"
  • RED (manual GDPR): booking reference + departure date in full (day, month, year) + full name as on the booking.
  • RETAIL / third party: booking reference + departure date in full + the ABTA number of the store.
Situation
What to say
RED scenario script
"Before I can access your booking details, I will need to confirm a few details with you. Can you confirm your booking reference please?"
"Can you confirm your departure date in full, including the day, month and year?"
"And can you confirm your full name as it appears on the booking?"
After it is passed: "Thank you for confirming those details. How can I help you today?"
Tip: Do not discuss the booking until all three pass.
Retail / third party
"Can you confirm your booking reference please?"
"Can you confirm your departure date in full, including the day, month and year?"
"What is the ABTA number for your store?"
After it is passed: "Thank you for confirming those details. How can I assist you today?"

Tip: Calm and brief. Do not apologise for the policy.
Caller is not the lead passenger

Tip: Read back letter by letter, digit by digit.

DISCOVER

Great service and great selling both start with great questions. Ask one, then listen.

Memorize these 6 questions

  • Who is travelling? Any children or infants?
  • Where and when? Are the dates flexible?
  • What matters most: price, comfort or convenience?
  • Special occasion? A birthday, an anniversary, a honeymoon?
  • Luggage and extras: will you need hold luggage? Any seat or meal preferences?
  • Budget: do you have a budget in mind so I can find the best fit?

ADD VALUE (UPSELL)

Sell by solving. If it does not help the customer, do not offer it.

Memorize the formula: Need, Link, Price, Ask, Exit

  • Need: find it in discovery. "You mentioned two young children..."
  • Link: "...so seats together mean you can sit beside them and relax."
  • Price simply: "It is [X] per seat each way, so [total] for the family."
  • Soft close: "Would you like me to add that for you today?"
  • Graceful exit if no: "Of course, no problem at all. You can always add it later."
  • One clear offer, one reason, one price, one question. Never repeat the same pitch after a no.

OBJECTIONS

An objection is the customer telling you what they need before they say yes.

Memorize the method: ACAA

  • Acknowledge: "I understand price matters." Never argue first.
  • Clarify: "May I ask what you are comparing it with?"
  • Answer: a short, honest answer or an alternative.
  • Advance: "Shall I go ahead with that?" or "What would you like to do?"
  • Avoid the word "but". Use "and" or "so".

"I understand price matters. Let me see what I can do. Are your dates flexible by a day or two, or would you consider a nearby airport? I can also look at whether you need all the extras so you only pay for what matters."
Tip: Find flexibility: dates, airport, extras, board basis.
"I saw it cheaper on another site."
"Thanks for telling me. Prices can vary by channel and what is included. May I ask what you saw, including bags and transfers? Let me check like for like and see the best I can offer."
Tip: Never criticise competitors.

ANGRY CUSTOMERS

Stay calm, stay professional, stay in control. Lower your voice and slow down.

Memorize the method: H.E.A.R.D.

  • Hear them out: let them vent, no interrupting, take brief notes.
  • Empathise: name the feeling. "I can hear how frustrating this has been."
  • Apologise: for the experience, not for blame. "I am really sorry this happened."
  • Resolve: clear options with timelines. "Here is what I can do..."
  • Deliver and follow up: do what you promised, note it, say what happens next.
Customer says
What to say
Shouting: "This is ridiculous! I have paid thousands!"
"You are absolutely right to be upset, and I am sorry. I am going to take ownership of this. Could you tell me what has happened so I understand it fully?"
Tip: Validate, own it, ask a question. Questions calm people down.
"This is the third time I have called!"
"I am sorry you have had to call again. I have read the notes, and I will personally make sure we get this resolved today. Here is what I am going to do: [action 1], [action 2], and you will hear back by [time]."
"You are useless!"
"I am sorry I have not been able to give you the answer you need yet. Give me a moment to check, and I will do everything I can to help."
Tip: Do not take it personally. Move to action.
"My holiday is ruined."
"I am so sorry. I know how much you were looking forward to this. Let us see what we can do to get things back on track. Can you tell me what has happened?"
Tip: Empathy first. Solutions after they feel heard.
"I want compensation!"
"I understand. Let me look at exactly what happened first, then I will explain what options are available. I can also raise a formal request for review."
Tip: Never promise compensation.
Swearing
1st: "I want to help you, and I would appreciate it if we could keep the conversation respectful."

RAPPORT + COMMON REQUESTS

British customers like warmth that is calm, polite and never pushy.

Memorize the British rules

  • Polite and understated: "please", "thank you", "lovely", "no problem at all".
  • Use Mr / Mrs / Ms [Surname] until they invite you to use their first name.
  • Small talk: one or two lines about the trip, then guide the call.
  • Never ask about age, job, income, marital status or health unless the booking needs it.
  • Best safe question: "Is it your first time visiting?"

ATCOM: EVERY CHANGE

Learn the master flow once, then only the differences.

The master flow (Amend Inclusive): used for add / remove passenger, date, destination, hotel, duration

  • 1. Tick the accommodation box, Menu, Amend Inclusive.
  • 2. Update passengers, rooms and dates if needed. Tip: choose the "any room" option to see everything available. Click search.
  • 3. Flight: keep the same flight (add pax) or choose the new one (date, destination, duration). Expand the Booking summary to match flight and accommodation. Proceed.
  • 4. Choose room type and quantity based on room occupancy.
  • 5. Passenger details. 6. Flight Extras (keep what exists, add what they want). 7. Services (same).
  • 8. TUI Care Foundation: check it is right (see table). Use "modify Item" to edit it.
  • 9. Errata if needed, full read back, Save, memo.

Integrated moves real money on a card. Non-integrated only moves money between bookings.

Memorize the difference

  • Integrated = money is actually taken from, or refunded to, the customer card.
  • Non-integrated = no money leaves or reaches the customer. You are moving money from one booking to another.
  • Pause the call recording before you take card details: "I am just going to pause the recording while I take your payment details for security."

AlwaysDeposit is non-refundable even if the charge is lower. Non-refundable extras are added. If TUI cancels: no fee.

Your training material also quotes fixed fees of 200 per person short haul and 250 per person long haul. Check which applies to the booking before you quote. Flight-only, hotel-only and tours have different charges.

Admin fee rule: compare what they have paid with (deposit + admin fees)

  • Paid MORE: do not collect the fee now. It comes out of what they already paid. Example: deposit 400, paid 1,000, fees 100: need 500, so collect nothing.
  • Paid LESS: collect the fee immediately. Say it is non-refundable even if they cancel later.
  • Low deposit outstanding: you can amend without collecting the outstanding deposit. Only collect the new admin fee.
  • After an amendment Direct Debits adjust automatically. Wait at least 5 days after their latest Direct Debit before refunds.
